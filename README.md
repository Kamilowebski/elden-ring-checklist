# Elden Ring Checklist

A Progressive Web App (PWA) for tracking your Elden Ring progress across different locations. Works offline, can be installed as an app on mobile devices, and tracks completion percentages.

## Features

✅ **Offline Support** - Works completely offline with Service Worker caching  
📱 **Mobile-Friendly** - Responsive design optimized for phones and tablets  
📊 **Progress Tracking** - Visual progress bars and percentage tracking  
📍 **Location-Based** - Organized by Elden Ring locations  
🎯 **Detailed Checklists** - Track Bosses, Treasures, Items, and NPC Quests per location  
💾 **Auto-Save** - Changes saved to browser storage automatically  
📤 **Export Data** - Download your progress as JSON  
🔄 **Reset Option** - Clear progress when needed  

## Locations Structure

Each location contains:
- **Bosses** - Boss encounters to defeat
- **Treasures** - Items and drops to collect
- **Items** - Collectible items and resources
- **NPCs** - NPC quests to complete

## How to Use

1. Visit the app URL
2. Click on any location card to view details
3. Check off items as you complete them
4. Progress is automatically saved
5. Overall progress is tracked at the top
6. Use "Export Data" to backup your progress

## Installation as App

### On Mobile (iOS/Android)
1. Open the app in your browser
2. Look for "Install" or "Add to Home Screen" option
3. The app will be installed and accessible from your home screen

### On Desktop (Chrome/Edge)
1. Click the install button in the address bar
2. App will open in its own window

## Offline Usage

Once installed, the app works completely offline:
- All data is stored locally
- No internet connection needed
- Changes sync when back online

## Data Management

- **Auto-Save**: Progress saves automatically to your browser
- **Export**: Click "Export Data" to download as JSON backup
- **Reset**: Use "Reset All" to clear progress (with confirmation)

---

**Note**: Data is stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser data will reset your progress. Always export your data for backup!
